Anchor the promise

Each date starts from the declared organizer wall time. UTC is derived from the browser’s current IANA timezone rules.

B

Expose the seam

The matrix labels the first scheduled window after an offset change, plus local times that repeat or never occur.

C

Compare evidence

Use the CSV or ICS fixture beside a booking page. A mismatch is evidence to investigate, not a claim about a vendor’s internal logic.

How does the audit handle ambiguous local time?

When clocks move back, one wall time can map to two real instants. Both are detected and the row is marked “Review”; the ICS fixture chooses the earlier occurrence and says so in its description. When clocks jump forward, a missing wall time is marked invalid and omitted from ICS.
