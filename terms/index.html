Terms of use

Use the results to check booking hours, not as a booking guarantee.

What this tool does

It converts declared weekly hours using the timezone rules in your browser. It highlights clock-change cases and creates expected CSV or ICS files.

What this tool does not do

It does not model scheduler buffers, notice periods, holidays, overrides, account settings, or proprietary availability rules.

Your review

Review repeated or missing local times. Test important booking flows in the scheduler before you publish availability.

Availability and warranty

The tool is free and provided as is. The maintainers do not promise uninterrupted availability or error-free timezone data.
