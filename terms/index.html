Use this tool as a preflight and evidence fixture, not as a booking guarantee.

Service

Availability DST Audit converts declared weekly wall-clock hours through the IANA timezone rules available in your browser. It highlights daylight-saving boundaries and exports expected-slot fixtures.

No vendor parity claim

Schedulers may apply buffers, minimum notice, overrides, holidays, account settings, or proprietary rules that this tool does not know. A difference is a reason to investigate; it does not by itself prove that a vendor is defective.
