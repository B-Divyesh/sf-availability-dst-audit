Policy / local-first

Privacy

Your availability configuration and generated fixtures stay on your device.

What is stored

After you run an audit, the organizer timezone, comparison timezone, date range, and weekly hours are saved in your browser’s local storage so the form can be restored. CSV and ICS files are generated in your browser and downloaded directly to your device.

What is not collected

The tool has no account system, advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, third-party scripts, or server-side fixture processing. We do not receive your schedule configuration or exports.

How to remove local data

Clear site data for availability-dst-audit.sociobot.in in your browser settings. Uninstalling the site or clearing local storage removes the saved configuration.

Infrastructure logs

The static hosting provider may process short-lived request logs for reliability and abuse prevention, such as IP address, requested path, and user agent. These operational logs are not used to profile you.

Contact and changes

Questions can be raised in the public source repository linked below. Material policy changes will be published here with a new effective date.
