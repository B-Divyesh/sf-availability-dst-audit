Privacy

Your hours and exported files stay in your browser.

What the app stores

After a real audit, the app stores the form settings in this browser. The sample demo uses a separate demo: storage key. Starting for real removes that sample key.

What the app sends

The audit runs in your browser. CSV and ICS files are created there. The app has no account, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Remove local data

Clear site data for availability-dst-audit.sociobot.in in your browser settings. This removes saved form settings and demo data.

Hosting logs

The static host may retain short request logs for reliability and abuse prevention. Those logs are not used for profiling.

Contact and changes

Raise questions in the public source repository. Material updates appear on this page.
